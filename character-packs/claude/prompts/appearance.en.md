# claude · appearance

Long warm copper-orange straight hair, inward-curving ends, side fringe, amber eyes and human ears. Dark chocolate ribbon and a small sunburst flower clasp. Ivory, chocolate and terracotta European-inspired dress with lace collar, puff sleeves, fitted buttons, fine gold chain and a restrained embroidered capelet. Dark strap shoes and an optional gold-cornered book. The chibi dress is simplified for legible movement while preserving this palette. No cat ears, crown or armor. Happy motion is a gentle clasp of hands and nod with feet planted, never stomping.
